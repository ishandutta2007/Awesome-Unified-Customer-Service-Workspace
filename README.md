# Awesome-Unified-Customer-Service-Workspace

# Top Unified Customer Service Workspace Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Omnichannel Inboxes, Ticket Management & Self-Hosted Helpdesks*  
**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **commercial customer service platforms** and **open-source projects** that unify customer conversations across email, chat, social media, and voice into a single agent workspace — from enterprise service consoles to self-hosted helpdesks and shared inboxes.

**Examples** include Salesforce Service Console, Zendesk Agent Workspace, Freshdesk Omnichannel, ServiceNow Service Operations Workspace, Kustomer Platform, Front Shared Inbox, Hiver, Gladly, Help Scout, and Intercom Inbox (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Unified customer service is one of the strongest open-source domains. **Chatwoot** leads with 37,000+ GitHub stars as the most complete open-source customer engagement suite — omnichannel inbox, AI agent Captain, help center, voice calls, and 400+ contributors . **Zammad** provides a 100% open-source, AGPLv3-licensed helpdesk with flexible configuration and multi-channel support . **FreeScout** delivers a super lightweight Zendesk/Help Scout alternative running on any PHP system . **osTicket** remains the veteran open-source ticketing system with millions of downloads . **UVdesk** brings a Symfony-based community helpdesk with e-commerce integrations . **erxes** offers an AGPLv3 experience operating system with omnichannel inbox, CRM, and AI agents . **Helpy** provides a modern helpdesk with knowledgebase and community discussions . This section is heavily expanded.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Salesforce Service Console](https://www.salesforce.com/)**  
  **Salesforce's unified agent workspace** — case management, omni-channel routing, knowledge base, and AI-powered recommendations built on the Salesforce platform. **Best for organizations already in the Salesforce ecosystem**.

- **[Zendesk Agent Workspace](https://www.zendesk.com/)**  
  **Zendesk's unified agent interface** — email, chat, voice, and social messaging in one workspace with AI-powered triage and macros. **Best for mid-market and enterprise support teams**.

- **[Freshdesk Omnichannel](https://freshdesk.com/)**  
  **Freshworks' omnichannel support platform** — ticketing, chat, phone, and social with AI-powered automation. **Best for SMBs and mid-market**.

- **[ServiceNow Service Operations Workspace](https://www.servicenow.com/)**  
  **ServiceNow's unified service workspace** — ITSM, customer service management, and field service in one agent console. **Best for large enterprises**.

- **[Kustomer Platform](https://www.kustomer.com/)**  
  **Customer service CRM platform** — unified customer timeline, omnichannel routing, and AI-powered automation. **Best for customer-centric brands**.

- **[Front Shared Inbox](https://front.com/)**  
  **Collaborative shared inbox** — email, SMS, chat, and social in one team inbox with internal comments and assignments. **Best for teams wanting email-first collaboration**.

- **[Hiver](https://hiverhq.com/)**  
  **Shared Gmail inbox for teams** — manage customer emails directly from Gmail with assignments, collision detection, and analytics. **Best for Gmail-centric teams**.

- **[Gladly](https://www.gladly.com/)**  
  **Customer service platform** — people-centered approach with unified customer history across channels. **Best for retail and e-commerce**.

- **[Help Scout](https://www.helpscout.com/)**  
  **Customer service platform** — shared inbox, knowledge base, and live chat with a focus on simplicity. **Best for small and mid-market teams**.

- **[Intercom Inbox](https://www.intercom.com/)**  
  **Conversational support platform** — live chat, email, and messaging with AI-powered Fin agent. **Best for SaaS and modern businesses**.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

### Full-Featured Omnichannel Platforms

- **[Chatwoot](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot)**  
  **The leading open-source customer engagement suite**, MIT licensed with **37,000+ GitHub stars** and **400+ contributors** . **Omnichannel support desk** — live chat, email, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, WhatsApp, Telegram, Line, SMS, and voice calls . **Captain AI agent** — resolves routine questions end-to-end, assists agents with suggestions, and delivers support in any language . **Help Center portal** — publish help articles and FAQs for self-service . **Collaboration features** — private notes, @mentions, labels, canned responses, auto-assignment, and agent capacity management . **Reports & insights** — live view, conversation/agent/inbox/label/team reports, and CSAT . **Self-hosted on your infrastructure** with full data ownership — 4+ CPU cores, 8GB RAM minimum, PostgreSQL 16, and Redis 7.0+ . **The de facto open-source Zendesk/Intercom alternative** . **Best for comprehensive omnichannel customer support**.

- **[Zammad](https://github.com/zammad/zammad)**  
  **100% open-source helpdesk and customer support platform**, AGPLv3 licensed . **Multi-channel support** — email, chat, telephone, and social media . **Flexible configuration** — from KISS principle to highly individual processes, automations, and third-party integrations . **Customer sovereignty over data and processes** — intuitive admin panel with maximum flexibility . **Owned by the Zammad Foundation**, independent of commercial providers . **Best for organizations wanting complete control with Swiss-army-knife flexibility**.

### Lightweight & Shared Inbox Platforms

- **[FreeScout](https://github.com/freescout-help-desk/freescout)**  
  **Super lightweight and powerful free open source help desk and shared inbox**, PHP (Laravel framework) . **Zendesk & Help Scout alternative without giving up privacy or locking into a service you don't control** . **Omnichannel** — email, WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, Slack, Live Chat, and more . **Unlimited support agents, tickets, and mailboxes** with no limitations . **Mobile-friendly, multilingual (33 languages), screen reader support** . **Runs on any system** — pure PHP/MySQL application deployable even on shared hosting . **3,898 GitHub stars and 607 forks** . **Best for lightweight, self-hosted shared inbox**.

- **[Helpy](https://github.com/helpyio/helpy)**  
  **Modern open-source helpdesk customer support application**, MIT licensed . **Features**: knowledgebase, community discussions, and support tickets integrated with email . **Best for teams wanting a modern helpdesk with community features**.

### Ticket Management & IT Support

- **[osTicket](https://github.com/osTicket/osTicket)**  
  **Widely-used open-source support ticket system**, GPL2 licensed . **Seamlessly integrates inquiries from email, phone, and web forms** into a simple multi-user web interface . **Manage, organize, and archive all support requests** while providing customer accountability . **Requirements**: PHP 8.2-8.4, MySQL 5.5+, Apache/IIS . **Attractive alternative to higher-cost and complex customer support systems** — simple, lightweight, reliable, and completely free . **Best for IT support and traditional ticketing workflows**.

- **[UVdesk Community](https://github.com/uvdesk/community-skeleton)**  
  **Fully-functional open-source helpdesk built on Symfony**, PHP-based . **Adaptability and extensibility for any trade** — inspect, modify, or utilize source code to build custom helpdesk solutions . **Support center portal, mailbox component, automation bundle, and API bundle** . **E-commerce integrations** — WordPress, Magento, Opencart, Prestashop, CS Cart, Joomla . **6,757 GitHub stars and 448 forks** . **Best for e-commerce and extensible helpdesk needs**.

### Experience Operating Systems

- **[erxes](https://github.com/erxes/erxes)**  
  **Open-source experience operating system (XOS)**, AGPLv3 licensed . **100+ modules. One source of truth.** — unifies frontline operations, revenue, and teams on one AI-native platform . **Team Inbox** — combine real-time client and team communication with in-app messaging, live chat, email, and forms . **Native AI steps** — classify, summarize, draft, decide without leaving the canvas . **Grounded in your workspace graph** — contacts, deals, tickets, documents, and audit trails . **Self-hostable, no vendor lock-in** . **Best for businesses wanting a unified CRM + support operating system**.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Customer service platforms handle sensitive customer data and may process PII. Self-hosted solutions require proper security hardening, access controls, and compliance with data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA).
- **License considerations**: Chatwoot uses MIT , Zammad uses AGPLv3 , FreeScout is open-source , osTicket uses GPL2 , UVdesk is open-source , erxes uses AGPLv3 , and Helpy uses MIT . Verify licensing against your use case before committing.
- **System requirements vary**: Chatwoot needs 4+ CPU cores, 8GB RAM minimum, PostgreSQL 16, and Redis 7.0+ . FreeScout runs on any system . osTicket requires PHP 8.2-8.4 and MySQL 5.5+ .
- The open-source ecosystem provides strong omnichannel inboxes, ticket management, and knowledge bases, but **managed infrastructure, AI-powered triage, and vendor-supported SLAs** remain primarily commercial offerings.
